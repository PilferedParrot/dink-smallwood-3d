# Tile forest decision brief — 2026-10-11

No third tree layout is implemented. The source art and collision boundaries disagree; a new density or spacing parameter cannot resolve that disagreement.

The RC4 exact coverage test inspected 38 forest-hard-edge screens: 12,200 of 38,401 boundary pixels cannot be covered by the original 94-pixel crown from any root simultaneously on forest art and source hardness. Even the optimal admissible root set leaves those gaps. Screen375 has 221/590 uncovered pixels and a maximum root distance 148px; screen144 has 420/1545 and 89px. The RC5 baseline census independently partitions 570 outdoor screens and 603 authored layers. Forest association accounts for 407,990 hard pixels and 47,528 potentially reachable hard-boundary pixels. Those boundary counts use a different, static-warp-connected reachability mask; they must not be substituted for the RC4 spacing denominator. There are 246,852 reachable boundary pixels with no attributed standing owner across all art families, so a forest decision cannot close the whole unseen-solidity defect.

Recommend the first direction, subject to Chris's approval of the changed picture. It preserves the authored routes and makes the measured hard boundary the construction input, eliminating the failed assumption that tree density can certify an edge. A bounded prototype must prove the difficult 375/144 mismatches and original-camera cost before a whole-world implementation. This is a proposal for the lead; no forest collision, art suppression, contour shell or new stand is enabled.

Evidence: `game/prototype/rc4_fir_stand_notes.md`, `game/prototype/rc4_fir_stand.json`, `tools/rc4_fir_stand_fit.py`, and the RC5 baseline JSON/summary recorded in the artifact ledger. Root causes: the first attempt applied an area-density heuristic to a boundary-coverage requirement; the second exposed a source art/mask mismatch under the admissible-root rule. Prior art is the existing exact forest-hardness compiler and native seam/texture mechanisms, plus the PyDink source editing model. Multiple-view forest reconstruction cannot recover depth from this single source atlas.

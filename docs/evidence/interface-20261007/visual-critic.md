## Per-frame notes
**title.png:** The original Dink logo is the first strong visual anchor, with adventure heading, highlighted start action and four menu buttons on the right. Instruction and release text read clearly against black. Checked logo fidelity against the supplied FreeDink title, text clipping, focus hierarchy, ornament placement and button styling. Findings 1–2 apply; the original logo retains its identity well.

**dialogue.png:** Mother is identified in yellow above a large cream dialogue line, with a strongly bordered Continue action below. Original vine motifs flank a stippled translucent panel. Checked text against the visible floor/wall background, line fit, action contrast and speaker hierarchy. All necessary text is legible in this frame; no additional finding.

**adventure.png:** A small location name tops the screen, with crosshair at center and a stone status strip below. Bow icon, Life 10/10, attack/defense/magic values, coins, experience and spell status read at full size. Checked against the supplied stone HUD artwork, icon placement, numeric contrast and bottom-edge fit. No additional UI finding; background world and weapon rendering are outside this review.

**equipment.png:** The original wooden Magic/Weapons/Items board dominates the modal, with fists selected by a bright border and a separate E on the bow. A readable title and instructions explain those states; the return action is distinct below. Checked the board against menu-01.png, slot alignment, selected/equipped distinction and text overlap. Board artwork and slots fit well. Finding 2 applies to the outer decoration.

**pause.png:** Ten evenly spaced labeled buttons fill a large dark panel. The first button has an unmistakable light focus border, and keyboard instructions remain readable below. Checked label fit, contrast, spacing and consistency with the title. Findings 1–2 apply; no unreadable or clipped action label is visible.

**settings.png:** Control bindings lead into labeled volume/text-size sliders, with sensitivity label near the viewport bottom and a visible scrollbar at right. The focused volume slider is distinguished by a gold outline. Checked text wrapping, percentages, slider contrast and the visible scroll affordance. Findings 2–3 apply. The sensitivity control continues below the visible viewport; this still alone does not establish a scrolling or accessibility failure.

**journal.png:** Yellow journal title, location, conversation heading and three quotations sit above a prominent return button. Checked readable text, grouping, attribution and distinction from dialogue presentation. Findings 2 and 4 apply; no visible text clipping.

**journal-back.png:** The pause menu is visible again with the same ten labels, first-row focus and footer. Checked it independently at full size for text, layout and rendering differences. Findings 1–2 repeat; the visible layout agrees with pause.png. A still does not prove navigation behavior.

**map-feedback.png:** A gold-bordered dark notification clearly says “I don't own a map yet.” above the world view; location label and the stone HUD remain distinct. Checked notification contrast, message hierarchy, overlap with other interface elements and HUD readability against the changed background. No additional UI finding.

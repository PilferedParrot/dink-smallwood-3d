# M3: what changed

October 2-3, 2026, on top of the tenth pass; released as version 0.3.0. One sheet:
`docs/images/m3.jpg`, the tenth pass on the left and M3 on the right. The full record is in `docs/DIRECTION.md`, the
section "Stacked trees and seam copies, and the rest of the 'still wrong' list".

**What you will see**
1. **No more floating trees.** Two of the tree pictures each hold two trees, one above the other. The game stood each
   pair as one tall card, so the far tree floated and the near one sank into the ground (east of Dink's house, 376).
   Each tree now stands on its own foot.
2. **One tree where the map meant one.** The original draws each screen alone, so the map repeats an object that
   crosses a screen's edge, once in each screen. The game drew every copy, so seam trees stood twice, and one stood
   whole over the cabin's roof on 251. Now each object is built once. Trees the original never shows are gone. So is
   a second copy standing a few pixels from the first. Nine trees that never stood when seen from the next screen now
   stand.
3. **Bridges have railings.** On the rope bridges that run east-west (404, 512, 693), the far railing stands at the
   deck's edge instead of lying on the planks, and the posts have thickness. From the bridge's own line the near
   railing no longer disappears. Arrows now hit a deck. The north-south plank bridges (448 and its kin) keep their
   ropes painted flat; standing ropes would float past the deck's ends (the reason is in DIRECTION.md).
4. **The castle's gatehouse and corners are solid.** The north gatehouse (80) is a block with its two buttresses
   instead of a flat picture. It shows half its width from the side, where it used to show a sliver. The corner
   pieces (16, 48) are solid too. A wall's far side shows the brick drawn for that side, and the merlons have
   thickness from above.
5. **The island's huts are round.** They were flat cards, slivers from the side; they are now solid round huts built
   from their pictures.
6. **The side flip is softer.** Walking round the cabin's tree on 251, a patch of canopy used to jump in front of the
   wall in one frame. It now dissolves in over a few steps.
7. The unused bridge model is removed.

**Still wrong, and what you may notice**
- The huts from the east or west are narrow eggs with a stretched, partly doubled picture on their flanks. Their depth
  follows this world's ground (a ground circle is drawn as an ellipse half as deep), as the castle's round towers do.
  There is a seam down each hut's back.
- The side flip's dissolve is a fine stipple. If you stand right on the wall's line, the tree stays half-stippled.
- The gatehouse's vaulted tops are a flat picture; its hidden faces streak on thin edges.
- The north-south bridges' ropes lie flat on the planks.
- The castle doors now sit in their walls and the drawbridge lies on the ground, but its chains are pale straps where
  the art draws dark iron links (visible through the original's camera too).
- Through the original's own camera, a few screens show trees that now stand behind a cabin roof where the original
  drew them over it (270), and the new 3D shadows fall in new places.

**Checks:** 121 tests pass, 1 skipped (95 at the tenth pass). Each fix has a test that failed on the old code or on a plausible wrong fix. Through the
original's camera, over all 570 outdoor screens, 100 screens are nearer the original's picture and 32 further. Each of
the 32 is traced to a known cause. Loading screens is no slower that the probe can see. Timings are approximate:
software rendering on a shared machine.
